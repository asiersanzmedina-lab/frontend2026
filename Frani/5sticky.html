Poems

🌌 1. Code of the Cosmos

I wrote a love song in binary,
Lines of logic wrapped in light,
My ship hums soft in lullabies,
Guided by stars through endless night.
You’re somewhere past Orion’s belt,
A dream encrypted in the void.
Each line I code is hope unspelled —
A space that time cannot avoid.

🌠 2. Nebula Kiss

We kissed beneath a gas cloud sky,
Our helmets fogged with cosmic dew.
The rings of Saturn turned nearby,
And Jupiter bowed just for you.
Your voice came through in static tones,
But in your eyes — whole galaxies.
You touched my glove; I felt your soul
In zero-G romantic ease.

🚀 3. Launch Sequence

Engines whisper your name in thrust,
As countdown echoes through the steel.
I left Earth chasing stardust trust,
Your memory my mission’s keel.
Each planet curves like poetry,
Each wormhole bends like dreams you wove.
In algorithms, I still see
The formula of your soft love.

🌌 4. The Orbit of You

I circle you, a lonely moon,
Forever caught in your bright sway.
No gravity could break this tune —
I orbit still, day after day.
You're my trajectory and fate,
Mapped in equations on my wall.
I slip through stars, and navigate
By how you make me feel at all.

👩‍🚀 5. In the Silence of Space

Out where the vacuum holds its breath,
Where time forgets to tick or turn,
I heard your heartbeat past the death
Of suns too bright for us to learn.
No sound, no wind, just frozen grace,
Yet still your whisper made its way —
A data pulse across deep space,
Decoded just to make me stay.

🌌 6. The Starship and the Rose

I built a ship with hands and hope, From circuits, gears, and golden dreams. I kept a rose within the scope, To show the stars what loving means.
The petals bloomed near Betelgeuse, Then danced toward Tau Ceti’s light. A robot hand, with love profuse, Watered it in endless night.

🪐 7. You, the Unknown Planet

I found a world not in the charts, A secret kept by comet’s veil. It had your breath, your voice, your heart — Your laughter echoed in its gale.
Its oceans bore your sleeping face, Its skies refracted all you are. I named it you — my hidden place, Discovered light-years past the stars.
